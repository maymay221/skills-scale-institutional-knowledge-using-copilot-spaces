# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define QA strategies, oversee test planning, and ensure quality standards are met throughout the project lifecycle. They work closely with development and release teams to identify defects early and maintain product excellence.

### Responsibilities
- Define and implement QA strategies and testing approaches
- Create test plans aligned with project phases and acceptance criteria
- Establish and monitor quality metrics and standards
- Lead testing activities and coordinate with development teams
- Identify and track quality risks and defects
- Ensure compliance with quality standards before release

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality products that meet customer expectations
- Reduce defects and rework through proactive testing
- Maintain clear visibility into quality status and risks

### Key Interfaces
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test design, defect triage, and quality acceptance
- **Project Managers**: Provide quality status updates and risk identification
- **Technical Architect**: Align testing strategies with system design
- **Release Manager**: Coordinate quality gates and release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and QA metrics dashboards
- Defect reports and quality status updates
- Sprint reviews and quality acceptance criteria discussions
- Test execution summaries and post-release quality reports

---

## Compliance & Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance & Risk Officers focus on regulatory compliance, risk assessment, and mitigation strategies. They ensure projects adhere to organizational policies, legal requirements, and industry standards while proactively identifying and managing risks.

### Responsibilities
- Assess regulatory and compliance requirements for projects
- Develop risk assessment frameworks and mitigation plans
- Monitor compliance with organizational policies and standards
- Identify and escalate compliance gaps and regulatory risks
- Support audit activities and documentation
- Partner with teams to implement risk controls

### Goals
- Ensure projects meet all regulatory and compliance requirements
- Minimize organizational risk exposure
- Maintain stakeholder confidence through transparent risk management

### Key Interfaces
- **Project Managers**: Collaborate on risk identification and mitigation planning
- **Product Managers**: Advise on regulatory implications of product decisions
- **Technical Architect**: Assess technical and security-related compliance needs
- **Documentation Specialist**: Ensure compliance documentation is current and accessible

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and compliance checklists
- Risk assessment reports and mitigation strategies
- Compliance updates and audit readiness summaries
- Policy and standards guidance documents

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define technical strategy, system design, and technical feasibility assessments. They provide technical leadership during planning phases and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical vision.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical strategy and system architecture
- Conduct feasibility assessments for product requirements
- Design system components and integration patterns
- Review technical decisions and provide architectural guidance
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Ensure technical consistency across projects and teams

### Goals
- Enable scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and long-term maintenance costs
- Align individual projects with overall technical vision

### Key Interfaces
- **Project Managers**: Advise on technical timeline and resource implications
- **Developers**: Partner on design reviews and technical implementation guidance
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility of product requirements
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Collaborate on testability and quality strategies

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture diagrams
- Feasibility assessments and trade-off analyses
- Code review guidance and architectural decisions
- Technical strategy updates and standards documentation

---

## Change Management Coordinator

### Role Summary
Change Management Coordinators manage organizational change activities, stakeholder communication, and adoption strategies. They ensure smooth transitions during project implementations and help teams and users adapt to new processes or systems.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change management and adoption strategies
- Plan and execute stakeholder communication campaigns
- Identify change impacts and resistance risks
- Coordinate training and support activities
- Monitor adoption metrics and address barriers to change
- Build engagement and support for project outcomes

### Goals
- Ensure successful adoption of project outcomes
- Minimize disruption and resistance to change
- Build organizational capability and stakeholder buy-in

### Key Interfaces
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate change activities within project timeline
- **Product Managers**: Align communication strategies with product positioning
- **Resource Manager**: Plan resource allocation for change activities
- **Documentation Specialist**: Ensure training and adoption materials are accessible

### Typical Communication
- Change management plans and stakeholder communication calendars
- Training materials and adoption guides
- Feedback and resistance management reports
- Post-implementation adoption metrics and insights

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Resource Managers oversee resource allocation, capacity planning, and team utilization across projects. They ensure teams have the skills and availability needed to deliver while optimizing organizational resource efficiency.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct capacity planning and resource forecasting
- Allocate resources across competing projects and priorities
- Monitor team utilization and workload balance
- Identify skill gaps and support development planning
- Manage resource conflicts and escalations
- Provide visibility into resource constraints and dependencies

### Goals
- Optimize resource utilization across projects
- Ensure adequate capacity for project success
- Build and maintain high-performing teams

### Key Interfaces
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate resource needs and allocation decisions
- **Development Team Lead**: Manage team composition and availability
- **Product Managers**: Balance resource allocation with prioritization
- **Compliance & Risk Officer**: Address compliance and risk-related resource needs

### Typical Communication
- Resource plans and capacity dashboards
- Allocation decisions and conflict resolutions
- Team utilization reports and skill gap analyses
- Workforce planning and development recommendations

---

## Documentation Specialist

### Role Summary
Documentation Specialists ensure all process documentation is current, accessible, and aligned with project delivery. They work across roles to capture best practices, lessons learned, and process improvements to support organizational learning.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and update process documentation
- Gather information from all roles to document activities and decisions
- Ensure documentation accuracy, clarity, and accessibility
- Capture and document lessons learned and best practices
- Support knowledge transfer and team onboarding
- Manage documentation standards and templates

### Goals
- Maintain comprehensive, current process documentation
- Enable organizational learning and knowledge sharing
- Support team efficiency through accessible guidance

### Key Interfaces
- **All Roles**: Collaborate to capture activities, decisions, and lessons learned
- **Project Managers**: Document project plans, status, and decisions
- **Compliance & Risk Officer**: Ensure compliance documentation is current
- **Change Management Coordinator**: Support training material development

### Typical Communication
- Process documentation and procedure guides
- Lessons learned summaries and best practice catalogs
- Documentation standards and templates
- Onboarding guides and knowledge base articles
